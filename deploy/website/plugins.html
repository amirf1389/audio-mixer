Plug-in downloads

Plug-ins from AMFB Audio for the racks of AmF X1. In the console: STORE, then DOWNLOADS. The files are also here, each with its size and checksum.

No plug-in is published for download yet. The console already includes its built-in effects: see Extensions and effects.

How a plug-in download is checked

The console offers only the plug-ins in AMFB Audio's signed list. A download comes over https, is compared with the size and SHA-256 in that list, and is saved into the console's own plug-in folder. Nothing is run by the download, and a file that does not match is deleted.

Plug-ins you already own load too: ADD VST on the STORE page takes their folder.
