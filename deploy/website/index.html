A digital mixer for every audio interface on your PC

Built for real audio setups

From a single USB microphone interface to several devices at once, AmF X1 opens each one with the best driver it has and shows you what is happening.

Drivers

ASIO first, Windows audio as backup

Opens your interface through its own ASIO driver. If the driver is busy or missing a side, the same hardware is used through Windows (WASAPI) so you still have sound.

Mixing

8 to 32 channels

Faders, pan, solo, mute, sends, group faders and scenes, with a lean engine that keeps CPU use low while idle.

Processing

EQ, dynamics and effects

Parametric EQ, compressor, noise gate, de-esser, mid/side width, real stereo polarity and an AI DSP rack with plugin store.

Metering

Phase, loudness, spectrum

Correlation to three decimals with phase angle, LUFS loudness, dual FFT analyser and per-device levels.

Languages

22 languages

English, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Persian, Arabic, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more, with accessibility options.

Privacy

Works offline

Licenses are checked on your PC with a digital signature. Nothing about your audio leaves your computer.
