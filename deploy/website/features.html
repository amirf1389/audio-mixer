Features

Everything in AmF X1 1.22.1.0 Beta, grouped by what you do with it.

Devices and drivers

Every interface in one list

USB interfaces, onboard sound, DACs, Bluetooth headsets and loopback devices are grouped by hardware, with read / write switches and a live level for each.

Same-hardware fallback

When an ASIO driver is held by another program, AmF X1 reads and writes the same device through its Windows driver instead of failing.

Low-latency ASIO

Your interface's own ASIO driver is used first, with automatic activation when the audio engine starts.

Mixing and processing

Console

Faders, pan, mute, solo, sends, group faders, scenes and presets, with smooth paging up to 32 channels.

Tone

Parametric EQ with an analyser overlay, mic EQ, dynamic EQ, auto-volume and a treble guard.

Dynamics and effects

Compressor, noise gate, limiter, de-esser, anti-feedback, chorus, lo-fi, auto-pan, harmony and vocal enhance.

Stereo image

Mid/side level and width plus left / right polarity that act on the real master signal.

Meters

Phase correlation

Goniometer and correlation meter to three decimals with the equivalent phase angle and a 10-step scale.

Loudness

Short-term and integrated loudness with true-peak for streaming targets.

Spectrum

A 20 Hz to 20 kHz dual FFT analyser for the master and any source.

Everyone

22 languages

Switch the interface language in Settings or with Alt+L; Persian, Arabic and Urdu switch to right-to-left.

Accessibility

Text size, high contrast, reduced motion, focus outlines and a screen-reader live region for every notification.

Light on your PC

Meters run at 30 frames per second (15 in low-power mode), hidden pages are not drawn, and low-power mode turns on by itself on battery.
