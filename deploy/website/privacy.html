Privacy

Short version: no tracking, no analytics, no cookies.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page) for security and removes them regularly.

Key requests

When you request a key you send your name, e-mail address, the plan and your computer's machine code. They are stored encrypted and used only to make and send your license key. Ask us at badriamirfakhri@gmail.com to see or delete them.

The app

Your audio never leaves your computer. The app checks for signed updates and, if you choose, opens WhatsApp or e-mail with a prepared message; nothing is sent without you pressing send.
