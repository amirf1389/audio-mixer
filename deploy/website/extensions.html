Extensions and effects

44 built-in effects and 2 extensions, all included with AmF X1. Nothing to buy here: switch them on in the console's STORE page.

Extensions

Program extensions run inside the console. They are installed with AmF X1; a new one arrives only as a package signed by AMFB Audio, through LOCAL UPDATE.
