Help and questions

Where is my machine code?

In AmF X1, open the LICENSE page: MACHINE CODE is at the top (five groups of four characters). It is a hash of the computer, not personal and not secret. MANAGE LICENCE ONLINE on that page opens this site with it filled in.

What is the difference between the licence key, the product key and the serial?

The licence key is the long AMIX1.… text. The product key is its short form, XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX. The serial is the 12-character licence id: with the e-mail it was issued to, it lets you look the licence up without the key.

How many computers can use one licence?

Each licence has its seats (1 to 4). MY LICENCE shows the computers it is active on; free one there to move the licence to another computer.

Does AmF X1 need the internet after activation?

No. The activation is signed and bound to that computer, and AmF X1 checks it offline for the whole licence term.

Why is DCA locked?

DCA groups are part of Pro and Studio Master. Basic runs the full console on 10 channels with DCA groups locked.

How do I pay for an upgrade?

Not on this site. REQUEST / UPGRADE opens WhatsApp with your edition and machine code; payment is agreed there.

Is the download safe?

The Downloads page checks the update list's signature with the AMFB Audio public key before it shows a file, and lists every file's SHA-256.

I typed my key into the wrong place. Is it stored?

No. This site keeps nothing in your browser. The server keeps the licence record (never the key) and which computers it is active on.
